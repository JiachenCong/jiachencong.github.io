Personal Interests

  • Cycling. In addition to cycling, I am also interested in bicycle repair and maintenance.

In college, I joined the tenor section of my department choir and was fortunate to win another first prize at a university-wide choral competition at Nankai University. Unfortunately, my musical skills have not aged as gracefully as I had hoped—they have been in steady decline ever since.

  • Playing badmintons: Part-time water cooler attendant and official bench manager.

  • Playing piano and guitar (learned them, partly forgot them, now trying to relearn them).
